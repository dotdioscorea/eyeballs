Remaining usage is now the default. Configure up to four usage/time rings per account, including weekly time remaining or elapsed. Each ring can use its own amount direction.

Compact mode shows the same metrics as horizontal bars and stays enabled across launches. Search, provider filters and account sorting are available above the account list. Cards include last updated times.

Widgets now share account selection code with the app and use an atomic summary cache. Choose a single account or a combination of accounts, compact bars or rings, metrics, amount direction and sorting. The accounts widget supports small, medium and large sizes.

Pull down to refresh accounts or an individual account. Usage history charts retain up to 90 days of successful readings on this device. The app checks every five minutes while open; background updates depend on iOS. Widgets use cached usage and do not keep the app running.
