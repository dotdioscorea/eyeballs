DESCRIPTION (1890/4000)
Keep track of your AI accounts in one place. Requota shows remaining usage, reset times and available credit balances, with widgets for a quick check throughout the day.

Connect your accounts directly on your iPhone or iPad, including multiple accounts from the same provider. Give each a name, organise them by workstream and see which has allowance left.

• Choose your view: rings, square tiles or compact bars. Search, filter, sort or arrange accounts in your own order.
• Customise each account: show usage remaining or used, choose which limits and reset countdowns appear, and set your colours.
• Add Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets: follow one account or use compact rows to see several together. Tap through to an account's details.
• Explore your history: compare accounts on interactive charts, check recent burn rates and time-to-zero estimates, and view activity heatmaps with daily breakdowns.
• Set your alerts: configure low-allowance warnings, reset reminders and reminders to use remaining allowance before a reset. Adjust notification rules for each provider.
• Follow account events: look back at observed resets, early resets, plan changes and banked reset activity where available.

Supported providers
Codex, Claude, Grok, Gemini CLI / Code Assist, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Cline, Kimi Code, Perplexity, Devin and Amp. Available details can include credit balances, extra spending and model-specific allowances.

Credentials are stored in your device's Keychain. Usage history is stored locally. The source code is public, with a link in Settings.

Requires an account with a supported provider. Available limits, balances and reset details depend on the provider and plan. History builds from readings collected by Requota. Background and widget refresh timing is controlled by iOS.

Requota is an independent app and is not affiliated with the listed providers.
